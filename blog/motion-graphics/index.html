Make the message move.

Motion gives information an order. In the Al-Waseet pieces, typography, images, app interfaces and brand colors work together to guide the viewer through a short message.

A different idea, a different visual language.

The Numo Media piece uses a quieter palette, dimensional objects and changing text arrangements. The visual language follows the idea instead of repeating the same treatment across every project.

Built around timing.

After Effects is my main working tool. I use timing, scale and transitions to connect the visual elements and keep attention on the message. Movement works best when it has a clear beginning, purpose and finish.
